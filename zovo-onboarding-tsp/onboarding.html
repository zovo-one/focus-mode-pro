It also works the way you'd hope. Blocking runs locally in the browser, so pages redirect the instant you try to open them, with no lag and no round trip to a server. Your work sites stay whitelisted, the Pomodoro timer keeps your rhythm, and your stats and streaks live only on your machine. Nothing about what you browse ever leaves your computer. It only asks for what blocking needs. Site access to catch a blocked page and send it to the calm screen, local storage for your lists and stats, alarms for the timer, and a quiet notification when a session wraps up. Nothing more.
